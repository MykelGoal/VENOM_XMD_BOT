# 🎬 CapCut Editing — PART 1 (Noob → Pro), Step by Step

Follow top to bottom. Don't skip. Each step = one action in CapCut mobile.

---

## ✅ BEFORE YOU OPEN CAPCUT
- Have your screen-recording clips ready (GitHub star+fork, session site, pairing, SESSION_ID copy).
- If you recorded one long clip, that's fine — we'll trim it.

---

## STEP 1 — New project & correct size (CRITICAL for TikTok)
1. Open CapCut → tap **New Project**.
2. Select your clip(s) → tap **Add**.
3. Bottom toolbar → tap **Ratio** → choose **9:16** (full vertical). ← do this first, always.
4. Tap **Format** (if there) → keep 9:16.

## STEP 2 — Trim the boring parts (pace = pro)
1. Tap your clip on the timeline (it turns white/outlined).
2. Drag the white edges to cut dead air at the start/end.
3. To cut the middle: move the playhead → tap **Split** → tap the bad piece → **Delete**.
4. GOAL: keep it moving. No section should sit still more than ~3 sec.

## STEP 3 — Speed up the slow bits (huge pro move)
1. Tap the clip → **Speed** → **Normal**.
2. For loading/typing parts, set speed **2x–4x**.
3. Keep the important moments (SESSION_ID appearing) at **1x**.
   → This alone makes a video feel professional instead of draggy.

## STEP 4 — The INTRO (first 3 seconds decide everything)
1. Move playhead to the very start.
2. Add a hook text: bottom toolbar **Text → Add text**.
3. Type: `Make your OWN WhatsApp bot 🕷️ (Part 1)`
4. Font: pick a **bold** one. Style tab → add **white text + black outline** (so it's readable on any background).
5. Size it BIG, place it upper-middle. Drag its length on the timeline to ~3 sec.
6. Tap the text → **Animation → In** → choose **"Bounce"** or **"Zoom in"**.

## STEP 5 — On-screen step labels (guides the viewer)
For each section add a short text (from your Part 1 script):
- `Step 1: Star ⭐ + Fork the repo`
- `Step 2: Open the session site`
- `Step 3: Link your WhatsApp`
- `Copy your SESSION_ID ✅`

For each: **Text → Add text → type → position bottom-third → add In/Out animation → trim to match the clip.**

## STEP 6 — Zoom / highlight important taps (pro polish)
1. When you click Star, Fork, or the SESSION_ID: tap the clip → this is where a **zoom** sells it.
2. Easiest way: tap clip → the frame → pinch-zoom in the PREVIEW at that moment, OR
3. Use **Keyframes**: tap clip → move playhead → tap the ◇ **keyframe** icon → pinch to zoom → move playhead forward → zoom back out. (This makes a smooth "punch-in" on the button.)

## STEP 7 — Add captions/subtitles automatically (accessibility = more watch time)
1. Bottom toolbar → **Captions → Auto captions**.
   - If you have voiceover already, it transcribes it.
   - If silent, skip — your step-text is enough.
2. Style captions: white, bold, bottom. Keep them short.

## STEP 8 — Music (sets the vibe, don't skip)
1. Bottom toolbar → **Audio → Sounds**.
2. Search a trending / upbeat tech beat (use the 🔥 "Trending" tab).
3. Add it → drag to start → trim to your video length.
4. Tap the music → **Volume** → set to about **20–30%** (so voiceover/steps aren't buried).
   - If you'll add my voiceover later, keep music low.

## STEP 9 — Transitions between clips (smooth = pro)
1. Tap the little **white □ box** between two clips on the timeline.
2. Pick a clean one: **"Pull in"**, **"Zoom"**, or **"Slide"**. Keep it FAST (0.3s).
3. Don't overuse — same transition throughout looks intentional and clean.

## STEP 10 — The OUTRO (the follow-bait)
1. At the end, add 3–4 sec.
   - Easiest: tap last clip → **Freeze** (freezes the last frame) → extend it, OR add a solid background.
2. **Text → Add text:** `Part 2: Deploy it LIVE 👉 Follow so you don't miss it`
3. Add a second smaller text: `Comment BOT for the link 👇`
4. Animate both **In: Zoom** / **Out: Fade**.
5. Optional: drop your logo — **Overlay → Add overlay → (your logo png)** → shrink to a corner.

## STEP 11 — Color pop (30-sec pro upgrade)
1. Tap a clip → **Adjust**.
2. Nudge: **Brightness +5, Contrast +10, Saturation +10, Sharpen +15.**
3. Do it on the main clips → footage looks crisp, not flat.

## STEP 12 — Final check before export
- ▶️ Watch it ONCE fully. Ask:
  - Is the first 3 sec a strong hook? ✅
  - Any part that drags? → speed it up.
  - Is text readable (outline)? ✅
  - Does it end with the Part 2 follow-bait? ✅

## STEP 13 — Export (correct settings)
1. Top-right → **Export**.
2. Resolution **1080p**, Frame rate **30** (or 60 if your recording was 60).
3. Export → then share to TikTok.
4. **Turn OFF the CapCut watermark:** at the end of your timeline there's an auto CapCut outro clip — tap it → **Delete** before exporting.
